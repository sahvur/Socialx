About Time Zones

UTC/GMT

Coordinated Universal Time - the basis for civil time around the world.

Daylight Saving

Some regions observe daylight saving time, shifting clocks forward during summer months.

Time Zones

The world is divided into 24 main time zones, each roughly 15 degrees of longitude apart.

International Date Line

An imaginary line that determines where the date changes - roughly follows the 180° meridian.
